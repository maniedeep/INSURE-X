====================================================================

THIS IS A CRITICAL DBMS REQUIREMENT.

Use MongoDB transactions/session.

Settlement must atomically perform:

1. Update claim status to SETTLED
2. Insert payment
3. Insert claim event
4. Insert audit log

All four must succeed together.

If ANY operation fails:

ROLL BACK EVERYTHING.

Never allow:

claim = SETTLED

without:

payment + event + audit record.

Create:

server/services/settlementService.js

startSession()
startTransaction()
operations
commitTransaction()

endSession()

PHASE 32 — PROBLEM STATEMENT
====================================================================

Use this project framing:

Traditional insurance information systems may maintain customer,
policy, claim, payment and investigation information across separate
workflows, making it difficult to track complete claim histories,
suspicious relationships, audit trails and risk-oriented reports.

INSURE-X proposes a centralized MongoDB-based insurance platform
that manages the policy and claim lifecycle while maintaining fraud
indicators, risk profiles, investigations, settlements and analytical
reports.
